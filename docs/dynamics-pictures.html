Pictures of dynamics

The pictures phase of the chaos package: cobwebs, orbits and return maps, bifurcation diagrams, escape-time pictures with honest pixels, attractors, L-systems and cellular automata, and frames that move a parameter.

Cobwebs

To iterate xn+1=f(xn)x_{n+1} = f(x_n)xn+1​=f(xn​) by eye: go up to the graph of fff, across to the diagonal y=xy = xy=x, and repeat. cobwebFigure(f, x0, n) draws that staircase with the graph and the diagonal. A fixed point is where the graph meets the diagonal; the staircase spirals in when ∣f′∣<1|f'| < 1∣f′∣<1 there and out when ∣f′∣>1|f'| > 1∣f′∣>1. It takes a float function or chaos's IteratedMap.

Orbits, return maps, bifurcations

orbitFigure draws xnx_nxn​ against nnn; returnMapFigure(values, { lag }) the pairs (xn,xn+k)(x_n, x_{n+k})(xn​,xn+k​), where a series that looks random can show it is a curve. bifurcationFigure(diagram) draws the long-run values from chaos's bifurcationDiagram, with marks at bifurcation points that chaos proves.

Escape-time pictures

Chaos classifies each pixel of the Mandelbrot set, a Julia set or Newton's basins as proved inside, proved outside, or undecided. escapeFigure(grid) keeps the three apart: inside dark, outside shaded by escape time, undecided in its own colour (orange by default), so the picture never claims more than was proved. Its status is certified.

Attractors, L-systems, automata

pointCloudFigure and attractor3d plot points in the plane or in space. segmentsFigure(lsystem.turtle(n)) draws a turtle path, marked exact when the angle is a multiple of 30° and chaos gives exact end points. spacetimeFigure(rows) draws an elementary automaton's run with time going down, and lifeFigure a Life pattern.

Moving a parameter

parameterFrames(values, build) builds one figure per value; framesSVG plays them. A cobweb as rrr runs through the period doublings of the logistic map is the classic example.
