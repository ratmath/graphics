Type something and look

A graph \(y = f(x)\), a curve \(F(x, y) = 0\), or a region. Names other than \(x\) and \(y\) become sliders with exact steps of \(\tfrac{1}{8}\). Drag the plot to pan; the buttons zoom by exact factors of 2.

While you drag a slider or the plot, you see the float sketch (blue). When you stop, the certified version (green) replaces it. The status chip says which one is on screen.
